Introduction

Welcome to UWM Course Review ("we," "our," or "us").

UWM Course Review is a student-focused platform designed to help University of Wisconsin-Milwaukee (UWM) students explore courses, read student reviews, share academic experiences, and make more informed decisions about their course schedules.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and disclose it, how we protect it, and what choices you have regarding your information.

By using UWM Course Review, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

Important: UWM Course Review is an independently developed student project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the University of Wisconsin-Milwaukee.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide, information generated through your use of our Services, and certain information from your browser or device.

1.1 Information You Provide

When creating an account, we may collect:

  • Username
  • Email address
  • Password (stored using password-hashing mechanisms)
  • Account registration and verification information

Our platform may require a valid UWM email address to register and verify your account.

User-Generated Content

When using our platform, you may voluntarily submit:

  • Course reviews and ratings
  • Course numbers and course titles
  • Feedback about instructors, assignments, and courses
  • Comments and discussion contributions
  • Information submitted through course-related tools

Reviews and other content submitted to public areas may be visible to other users and website visitors. Your username may appear alongside your submissions.

Communications

If you contact us for assistance, report an issue, or provide feedback, we may collect your email address and the contents of your communication.

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

When accessing our Services, certain technical information may be collected:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Device and operating system information
  • Date and time of access
  • Pages visited and website interactions
  • Technical logs and diagnostic information
1.3 Cookies and Similar Technologies

We may use cookies and similar technologies to support website functionality, maintain authentication sessions, remember preferences, and protect against unauthorized access.

You can configure your browser to reject cookies. However, certain features, including account authentication, may not function properly without them.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for purposes related to operating, maintaining, and improving UWM Course Review.

Providing Our Services
  • Creating and maintaining user accounts.
  • Verifying user email addresses.
  • Allowing users to search for courses.
  • Displaying course reviews and ratings.
  • Enabling users to submit reviews and discussions.
  • Providing course-related calculation tools.
Improving Our Platform
  • Understanding how users interact with our website.
  • Identifying technical issues.
  • Developing additional features.
  • Improving website performance and functionality.
Security and Platform Integrity
  • Detecting suspicious activity.
  • Preventing spam and fraudulent accounts.
  • Enforcing website guidelines.
  • Protecting users and our platform.
Communication
  • Sending account verification emails.
  • Responding to support requests.
  • Providing important service updates.

3. How We Share Your Information

We respect your privacy and do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3.1 Public Reviews and Contributions

Reviews, ratings, and other content submitted to public areas of UWM Course Review may be visible to other users and visitors.

Your username and submitted content may be displayed alongside your reviews. Your email address and password are not intended to be displayed publicly.

Please exercise caution when submitting information because public content may be copied or shared by others.

3.2 Service Providers

We may rely on third-party service providers to support our platform, including:

  • Website hosting providers.
  • Email delivery services.
  • Database and infrastructure providers.
  • Website security and maintenance services.
3.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable laws, respond to valid legal requests, investigate suspected fraud, or protect the rights and safety of our users.

3.4 Business Transfers

If ownership or operation of UWM Course Review changes, user information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to applicable privacy laws.

4. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our Services, fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, resolve disputes, maintain security, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

  • Account information may be retained while your account is active.
  • Reviews and discussions may remain published while available on the platform.
  • Technical logs may be retained for security and troubleshooting.
  • Verification information may be retained as needed for account security.

If you request account deletion, we will take reasonable steps to delete or anonymize personal information associated with your account, subject to applicable legal requirements and legitimate security needs.

5. Your Privacy Rights and Choices

Depending on your location and applicable privacy laws, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information.

Access

Request information about the personal information we maintain about you.

Correction

Request that inaccurate or incomplete personal information be corrected.

Deletion

Request deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable exceptions.

Withdrawal of Consent

Withdraw consent where we rely on consent to process certain information.

Account Management

Request assistance with updating or managing your account information.

To exercise these rights, contact us using the information provided in Section 11. We may need to verify your identity before processing certain requests.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss, or destruction.

These measures may include:

  • Password hashing.
  • Account authentication and verification.
  • Access restrictions.
  • Website security practices.
  • Routine maintenance and monitoring.

However, no website, internet transmission, or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Age

UWM Course Review is intended for individuals interested in higher education and university coursework.

Our Services are not directed toward children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 13 without appropriate authorization, we will take reasonable steps to delete that information.

8. Third-Party Websites and Services

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, resources, or services.

These services operate independently and may have their own privacy policies and practices. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of external websites.

We encourage users to review the privacy policies of third-party services before providing them with personal information.

9. University Affiliation and Educational Information

UWM Course Review is an independent platform intended to help students share academic experiences and explore course information.

  • We are not an official University of Wisconsin-Milwaukee website.
  • We do not represent the university or its faculty.
  • We do not access official university student records or enrollment systems.
  • Reviews and ratings represent individual user opinions and experiences.

Users should not submit confidential educational records, student identification numbers, grades, or other sensitive academic information through public reviews or discussions.

Information submitted to our platform should not be considered an official university record or substitute for information provided directly by UWM.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes to our website, services, privacy practices, or applicable legal requirements.

When changes are made, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the beginning of this document.

If significant changes are made, we may provide additional notice through our website or other appropriate communication methods.

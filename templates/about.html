Why we built it

A clearer picture of the workload.

Course catalogs tell you what a class covers, but they may not tell you how students experience its pace and workload. This project brings course information and student feedback together to help you make a more informed choice.

Understanding the ratings

Students rate both difficulty and time commitment on a five-point scale. These scores summarize individual experiences, not a promise of how a course will feel for everyone.

1

Lower rating
Easier material or less time outside class.

5

Higher rating
More challenging material or more time outside class.
